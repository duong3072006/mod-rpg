## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH LISTING (Modrinth / CurseForge)

### One-Line Summary
> **5 colossal multi-phase GeckoLib bosses, 7 Mythological Titan encounters, an 18-floor scaling Abyssal Spire Dungeon, massive Overworld structures, and progressive RPG gear for Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric.**

### Description

**Abyssal Spire RPG** brings colossal multi-phase boss encounters, an 18-floor scaling dungeon, massive Overworld temples and fortresses, and progressive RPG weapons and armor to **Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric**—without adding TerraBlender overhead.

#### 🐉 5 Colossal Multi-Phase GeckoLib Bosses
- Challenge **Leviathan**, **Spirit Hydra** (4 severable & regrowing heads), **Cerberus** (3-headed split burst), **Nightmare Knight**, and **Jörmungandr** (multi-segment world serpent)—each featuring custom GeckoLib `4.8.3` animations, emissive glowmasks, multipart hitboxes, Phase 2 transformations, and independent JSON configs (`vfxQuality = MINIMAL` and `terrainDamageEnabled = false` by default for server safety).

#### 🏰 18-Floor Abyssal Spire Dungeon & RPG Progression
- Climb the **18-floor Abyssal Spire Dungeon** using `/abyss menu`, `/abyss enter <1..18>`, `/abyss exit`, and `/abyss stats`.
- Forge and wield **18 elemental RPG weapons** and **7 full armor sets (28 pieces)** while exploring 6 massive Overworld structures (`Grand Citadel`, `Titan Colosseum`, `Ancient Spire`, `Catacomb Fortress`, `Obsidian Bastion`, `Mythic Temples`) and 4 core RPG biomes.
- **100% English & Vietnamese localization** (`261` keys).

### Requirements & Compatibility

| Requirement | Version | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minecraft** | `1.21.1` | Client & Dedicated Server (`*`) |
| **Mod Loader** | Fabric Loader `>= 0.16.0` | Verified on `0.20.1` |

| **Fabric API** | `>= 0.116.0` | **Required external download** |
